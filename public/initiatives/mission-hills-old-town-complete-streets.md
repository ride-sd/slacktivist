# Information

SANDAG and City of San Diego leadership have let the **Mission Hills & Old Town Complete Streets Project** stall for over a decade, even though it's one of the highest-ranked projects in SANDAG's 2013 Regional Bikeways Early Action Program. The project would finally finish safe walking and biking connections on **West University Avenue, San Diego Avenue, and Congress Street**.

- SANDAG recently returned **$1 million** in state and federal funding for this project — money that would have finished a design that's been sitting on the shelf since 2015. ([KPBS story](https://bit.ly/4sww0h3))
- SANDAG is building a sidewalk and protected bikeway on Washington Street right now, but it **ends abruptly**, forcing people walking and biking into traffic with impatient, speeding drivers.
- Without this project, there's no safe connection to the **Old Town Transit Center**, the University Avenue protected bikeways, or **4 schools** — including the Grant K-8 campus, which just relocated to Congress Street & San Diego Avenue, putting even more students directly in the path of this gap.
- Bike commuter, father, and grandfather **Marc Woolf** was fatally struck while riding home from work on the Old Town section in 2021.
- The City of San Diego and Councilmember Stephen Whitburn have asked SANDAG to resubmit this project for the same Active Transportation Program (ATP) grant it previously won. SANDAG is refusing — and the ATP Cycle 8 application is due **November 20, 2026**, with the next opportunity not coming for another two years.

SANDAG and the City could jointly apply to build the already-designed improvements as a low-cost, five-year **Quick Build** trial with continuous community input. Help us tell decisionmakers to reapply for this funding before the window closes.

# Content

Dear SANDAG, City, County, State, and Federal Leadership,

I am writing to urge you to bring the money back into our community for the Mission Hills & Old Town Complete Streets Project. I do not understand how SANDAG can return millions of dollars needed to finish this, and other Active Transportation projects, on its High Injury Network. This is one of the highest ranked projects on the 2013 Regional Bikeways Early Action Program.

Now, SANDAG is refusing the community's and City Councilmember Whitburn's request to resubmit this project by November 20th, even though this project was previously successful for the same ATP grant. SANDAG leadership, please work with City of San Diego leadership on a joint Quick Build ATP application to immediately make West University Ave, San Diego Ave, and Congress St safe for the students at 4 schools who risk their lives every day just trying to learn and grow safely with their friends.

SANDAG has shared designs for this project with our community for more than a decade. But when SANDAG CEO Mario Orso took over, the community stopped receiving updates until we learned from a KPBS article last month that SANDAG returned the money for this project with no intention of getting it back. The community is desperate for the SANDAG designs to get implemented as Quick Build to improve our dangerous streets. This can be a win for SANDAG and the City of San Diego to develop low cost and effective solutions with a 5 year trial period and continuous community engagement that will make sure the best permanent project is ultimately completed. With the ATP only available every 2 years, now is the time to reapply and get that money back.

Our neighbors have been working with SANDAG on getting this project built for our community since 2013, and every year more people get hurt riding, driving, and walking on these sections. When the City approved the Hillcrest Focused Plan Amendment in July 2024, we expected this project to be completed on West University Avenue during its repaving in 2025, but instead it is just as dangerous as before. SANDAG and the City of San Diego are making improvements on either side, which were desperately needed and are greatly appreciated, but if you leave these dangerous gaps it will discourage biking or walking as an option because the infrastructure you planned will remain missing, betraying trust in the government to deliver on its plans.
