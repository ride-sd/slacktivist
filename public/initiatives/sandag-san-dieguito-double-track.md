# Information

On **November 20, 2026**, the SANDAG Board of Directors will vote on [Agenda Item 4](https://pub-sandag.escribemeetings.com/Meeting.aspx?Id=e8a50908-5e5c-4442-b332-5d83f171a06c&Agenda=Agenda&lang=English): a Transportation Committee recommendation to approve a **$105,000,427 FY 2027 budget amendment** and **award the construction contract** for the [San Dieguito Lagoon Double Track project](https://www.sandag.org/sddt) (SDDT, Capital Improvement Program Project No. 1239822).

**What the project does:**
- Replaces the existing **more-than-100-year-old wooden trestle bridge** over the San Dieguito Lagoon with a new, modern double-track bridge, elevated above the 100-year floodplain to withstand storms, king tides, and sea level rise
- Adds a second track along the LOSSAN Corridor between Solana Beach and Del Mar, reducing the single-track bottleneck that forces Amtrak Pacific Surfliner and COASTER trains to wait for each other
- Builds a **special events platform at the Del Mar Fairgrounds**, giving riders a safe, direct transit option straight to fairgrounds events — most notably the San Diego County Fair — cutting vehicle traffic and parking congestion on event days

**Why the timing matters:**
This project has already been through a decade of environmental review, design, and permitting. It is fully funded for construction. Every year of delay means a deteriorating century-old bridge stays in service longer, construction costs continue to climb, and riders and fairgoers wait longer for the benefits. Some local residents and the City of Del Mar have pushed to delay this vote, but further delay only increases risk and cost without changing the outcome — the bridge still needs replacing and the corridor still needs double tracking regardless of how a separate, long-term Del Mar bluff realignment decision is eventually resolved.

**Make your voice heard live:** In addition to emailing the Board, you can join the meeting and give public comment yourself on **Friday, November 20 at 9 a.m.** The Board of Directors meets in person at the SANDAG Boardroom (1011 Union Street, San Diego) or virtually via Zoom — [join the Zoom meeting here](https://us02web.zoom.us/j/85641178468).
