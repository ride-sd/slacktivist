# Information

SANDAG wants to prioritize a $1 billion+ streetcar over far cheaper, more transformational projects. The Uptown Streetcar seems like a great idea at the surface, but it falls apart in the context of current reality. It is an incredibly expensive project that wouldn't even be faster than the busses it's replacing. A billion dollar vanity project SHOULD NOT be SANDAG's priority...

What SANDAG should do instead:

- **Improve Current Buses** - This money can go toward improving the bus infrastructure, speeding up and improving the reliability of all of the buses along this corridor
- **Study Better Lines** - This project takes away needed money to properly study rail lines in Mid-City and Uptown

# Content

Dear SANDAG Board Members,

I am writing to urge you to shift your spending on the planned Balboa Park Perimeter/Uptown Streetcar.

While advancing rail projects should be a serious priority in any rail plan, this particular project is a step in the wrong direction. $1 Billion dollars on a project that fails to serve any neighborhood well is an absolute nonstarter.

Every single neighborhood this line would stop in deserves high quality rapid transit, but this streetcar falls incredibly short of that. Streetcars are not the modern day solution. They share lanes with cars, stop far too frequently, and can end up even slower than the buses they are meant to replace.

While streetcars do carry a historic nostalgia that a lot of people desperately want to bring back, the environment they originally operated in no longer exists. There is so much traffic that forcing a train to be stuck in it is a nonstarter. It would be like a Trolley line, but with all of the advantages stripped from it while retaining the downsides.

This project was conceived 15 years ago, based on the Portland Streetcar, and planned to SUPPORT projects like a Mid City rail line and the Purple Line rather than be the spine. No modern streetcar has come close to replicating the Portland Streetcar's success, and the Mid City rail line has been completely removed from plans, while the Purple has been severely cut back and the mode downgraded.

My Ask:

Please prioritize other projects instead, and remove this streetcar project from regional planning. This project no longer makes any sense. This $1b+ could instead go an incredibly far way toward studying the original Mid City rail line and studying the Purple Line as a high speed metro from the border to Sorrento Valley. Additionally, investing this money into Rapid buses on the corridors this streetcar would serve would actually go a long way to improve transit in the area.

I am committed to staying engaged on this issue and hope SANDAG will come to their senses about how bad of a project this is.

Thank you for your consideration.
